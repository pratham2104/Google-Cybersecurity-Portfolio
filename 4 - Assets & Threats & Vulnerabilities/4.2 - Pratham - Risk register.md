# Risk Register

## Operational Context

The bank operates in a coastal community with one of the lowest crime rates in the region. It employs 100 staff on-site and 20 remote workers. Its portfolio includes 2,000 individual accounts and 200 commercial accounts. Brand visibility is enhanced by sponsorship of a local professional sports team and partnerships with ten nearby businesses. Federal regulations require the bank to maintain daily liquidity and rigorously protect customer data and funds.

| **Asset** | **Risk**                        | **Description**                                                    | **Likelihood**<br>(Rare 1, Likely 2, Certain 3) | **Severity**<br>(Low 1, Moderate 2, Catastrophic 3) | **Priority**<br>(L × S) |
|-----------|---------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Funds     | Business email compromise       | An employee is deceived into revealing credentials via phishing.  | 2                                             | 2                                                   | 4                       |
| Funds     | Compromised user database       | Stored customer records lack proper encryption safeguards.         | 2                                             | 3                                                   | 6                       |
| Funds     | Backup data exposure            | A backup server is misconfigured and publicly reachable on the Internet. | 2                                         | 2                                                   | 4                       |
| Funds     | Physical theft                  | The bank vault is accidentally left unlocked overnight.            | 1                                             | 3                                                   | 3                       |
| Funds     | Supply chain interruption       | Delays in critical hardware or software deliveries from vendors.   | 1                                             | 2                                                   | 2                       |
