## Scenario 

You are a cybersecurity analyst working for a multimedia company that offers web design services, graphic design, and social media marketing solutions to small businesses. Your organization recently experienced a DDoS attack, which compromised the internal network for two hours until it was resolved.

During the attack, your organization’s network services suddenly stopped responding due to an incoming flood of ICMP packets. Normal internal network traffic could not access any network resources. The incident management team responded by blocking incoming ICMP packets, stopping all non-critical network services offline, and restoring critical network services. 

The company’s cybersecurity team then investigated the security event. They found that a malicious actor had sent a flood of ICMP pings into the company’s network through an unconfigured firewall. This vulnerability allowed the malicious attacker to overwhelm the company’s network through a distributed denial of service (DDoS) attack. 

To address this security event, the network security team implemented: 
- A new firewall rule to limit the rate of incoming ICMP packets
- Source IP address verification on the firewall to check for spoofed IP addresses on incoming ICMP packets
- Network monitoring software to detect abnormal traffic patterns
- An IDS/IPS system to filter out some ICMP traffic based on suspicious characteristics

As a cybersecurity analyst, you are tasked with using this security event to create a plan to improve your company’s network security, following the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework (CSF). You will use the CSF to help you navigate through the different steps of analyzing this cybersecurity incident and integrate your analysis into a general security strategy:

1. Identify security risks through regular audits of internal networks, systems, devices, and access privileges to identify potential gaps in security. 
2. Protect internal assets through the implementation of policies, procedures, training and tools that help mitigate cybersecurity threats. 
3. Detect potential security incidents and improve monitoring capabilities to increase the speed and efficiency of detections. 
4. Respond to contain, neutralize, and analyze security incidents; implement improvements to the security process. 
5. Recover affected systems to normal operation and restore systems data and/or assets that have been affected by an incident.

## Summary  
The internal network of a multimedia company experienced a **Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS)** attack, which caused a two-hour outage. The attack was initiated through a flood of **ICMP packets** entering via an unconfigured firewall. This led to service disruption across web design, graphic design, and social media platforms. The incident was mitigated by blocking incoming ICMP traffic and taking non-critical services offline. Post-resolution, the cybersecurity team implemented firewall rules, source IP validation, network monitoring tools, and an IDS/IPS system.

---

## Identify  
- **Type of Attack**: Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) via ICMP Flood  
- **Vulnerability**: Misconfigured firewall allowing unrestricted ICMP traffic  
- **Systems Affected**: Internal network infrastructure, application servers, internal communication systems  
- **Assets Impacted**: Network bandwidth, client service availability, internal communications  
- **Business Processes Disrupted**: Web development pipelines, graphic production, and client social media content delivery  

---

## Protect  
To strengthen the network against similar attacks:
- **Firewall Hardening**: Rate-limit and block unsolicited ICMP packets.
- **Source IP Validation**: Implement IP spoofing detection and rejection mechanisms.
- **Access Controls**: Refine policies to restrict protocol-level access to trusted entities only.
- **Training and Awareness**: Educate IT and DevOps teams on firewall configurations and DDoS prevention.
- **Update Procedures**: Include firewall configuration reviews in change management processes.
- **Security Tools**: Deploy Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS) and advanced firewalls as preventive layers.

---

## Detect  
To identify potential threats proactively:
- **Network Monitoring**: Use tools such as Wireshark, Nagios, or SolarWinds to monitor real-time traffic anomalies.
- **IDS Deployment**: Integrate intrusion detection systems like Snort or Suricata.
- **SIEM Tools**: Centralize alerts and logs using Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) platforms.
- **Behavior Analysis**: Set thresholds for ICMP activity and monitor deviations.
- **Log Review**: Regularly analyze logs for early indicators of a coordinated flood attack.

---

## Respond  
In the event of a similar future incident:
- **Containment**: Block the attack vector (ICMP) and isolate affected systems.
- **Communication**: Notify internal teams and impacted stakeholders about the incident.
- **Investigation**: Collect logs, trace IP origins, and assess attack scale.
- **Mitigation**: Use temporary blacklists, geo-blocking, and scrubbing services like Cloudflare or Akamai.
- **Documentation**: Perform root cause analysis and document actions taken.
- **Process Improvement**: Update incident response plan and train staff on execution.

---

## Recover  
Post-incident steps to restore normalcy:
- **Service Restoration**: Re-enable critical and non-critical services once safe.
- **Configuration Checks**: Validate updated firewall and monitoring settings.
- **Data Integrity Review**: Confirm no corruption or tampering occurred during the attack.
- **Postmortem**: Conduct a team debrief and compile lessons learned.
- **Redundancy Planning**: Evaluate adding DDoS protection services and fallback connections.
- **Stakeholder Notification**: Communicate recovery status to clients and leadership.

---

## Reflections / Notes  
- Firewall misconfiguration was the root cause of this exploit.
- Proactive monitoring and validation policies are now prioritized.
- Consider deploying third-party DDoS mitigation services for added resilience.
