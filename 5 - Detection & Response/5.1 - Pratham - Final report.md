## Description
Documenting a ransomware attack on a small U.S. healthcare clinic that disrupted operations and encrypted patient data.  

## Tool(s) used
No detection tools were specified in the scenario. Incident was identified through employee reports and ransom note.  

## The 5 W's

- **Who caused the incident?**  
An organized group of unethical hackers targeting healthcare and transportation organizations.  

- **What happened?**  
Attackers used phishing emails with malicious attachments to deploy ransomware, encrypting critical patient data and shutting down business operations.  

- **When did the incident occur?**  
Tuesday morning at approximately 9:00 a.m.  

- **Where did the incident happen?**  
At a small U.S. healthcare clinic specializing in primary-care services.  

- **Why did the incident happen?**  
The clinic’s employees fell victim to phishing emails, allowing attackers to install malware and encrypt files for ransom.  

## Additional notes
The clinic had to shut down systems and contact outside organizations for assistance. This highlights the need for employee security awareness training, stronger phishing defenses, and a comprehensive incident response plan.
